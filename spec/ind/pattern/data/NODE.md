# IND.PATTERN.DATA: Pattern method declarations and reference datasets

## Purpose

The feature-pattern pack's manifest declarations (recognition and summary method IDs, maintainer) and its reference datasets: a CAD part with at least 200 holes in linear, circular and grid patterns plus decoys, with the expected grouping.

## Scope

**In:**
- Manifest entries: method IDs `pack.pattern.<step>.<method>@<semver>` and maintainer
- A generated STEP part (or B-rep fixture) with at least 200 holes in linear, circular and grid patterns, plus decoys differing in size or orientation
- Expected grouping, kinds, parameters and member order in `fid.pack.expected@1`

**Out** (and which node owns it instead):
- The harness (owned by `IND.CONTRACT.VERIFY`)
- Algorithms under test (owned by `IND.PATTERN.FIND`, `IND.PATTERN.INST`, `IND.PATTERN.SUM`)

## Requirements

- **REQ-IND.PATTERN.DATA-001** The feature-pattern pack manifest shall declare its recognition and summary methods as versioned method IDs and shall name at least one maintainer.
- **REQ-IND.PATTERN.DATA-002** The pack shall ship a reference part with at least 200 holes in linear, circular and grid patterns plus decoy features, with the expected grouping and member order.
- **REQ-IND.PATTERN.DATA-003** The reference part shall be generated by a seeded script whose version and seed are recorded, so it can be regenerated identically.

## Interfaces

- **Offers:** manifest fragment and dataset folder for the pattern pack.
- **Consumes:** `IND.CONTRACT.MANIFEST` schema; `IND.CONTRACT.VERIFY` format.

## Decomposition hints

GEN (reference part generator), DECL (manifest declarations).

## Open questions

- Generating a STEP file needs a CAD writer; if none exists in IO at P3, the fixture may be a GEO-native B-rep instead.
