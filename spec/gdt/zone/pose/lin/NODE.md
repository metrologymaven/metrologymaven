# GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN: Per-shape linearisation and active-set callbacks

## Purpose

Poses the zone-placement problem for each `ZoneShape` in the form the KRN sequential solver and certificate framework expect: a pose increment over only the free DOFs, a linearised constraint per point (LP rows for plane, line and offset zones; second-order cones for cylinder, circle and sphere zones), the objective "minimise zone size", and the active-set equations that recompute the optimum exactly from the critical points. This is the only place where zone geometry meets the solver.

## Scope

**In:** pose increment x over free DOFs (small rotation about the DRF origin, then translation, in the DRF axes); per-shape implementations of `LinearisableProblem` (build the local LP/SOCP about the current pose; evaluate the true objective) and `ActiveSetProblem` (equal-deviation system at the critical points); offset-zone linearisation through `DeviationOracle` normals; the `ExtraConstraints` hook (additional linear or cone rows supplied by MATL, GROUP, COMP and MOBSRCH).
**Out:** the iteration loop and stop rules (owned by `KRN.SOLV.SEQ`); certificate checking (owned by `KRN.SOLV.CERT`); the solve driver and result packaging (owned by `GDT.ZONE.POSE.SOLVE`).

## Requirements

- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-001** The pose increment shall have one variable per free DOF, in the fixed order tx, ty, tz, rx, ry, rz restricted to the free set, and constrained DOFs shall not appear as variables.
- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-002** For two-plane, two-line and offset shapes, each point shall contribute two linear rows −s/2 ≤ g_i + J_i·x ≤ s/2 (with g_i the current signed distance or d_i − c and J_i its gradient), and the objective shall minimise s.
- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-003** For cylinder, circle and sphere shapes, each point shall contribute one second-order cone ‖A_i x + b_i‖ ≤ s/2 linearised about the current pose, and the objective shall minimise s.
- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-004** For offset shapes, the gradient J_i shall be computed from the oracle's foot-point normal n_i as n_iᵀ·∂p_i/∂x at the current pose, and the true objective shall be re-evaluated with fresh oracle deviations at each trial pose.
- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-005** The `ActiveSetProblem` for each shape shall recompute s and the pose from the active points by a direct solve, and the recomputed s shall equal `GDT.ZONE.ZTYPE` usage at that pose within 1 ulp of s.
- **REQ-GDT.ZONE.POSE.LIN-006** Extra rows from the `ExtraConstraints` hook shall be appended after the shape rows in the order supplied, and shall use the same variable layout.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given fixture `fixtures/gdt/zone/lin_planes.json` (two-plane zone, free {tz, rx, ry}), when the local LP is built at the identity pose, then its rows and objective match the fixture's expected matrices bit-for-bit.
- **AC-2** Given a finite-difference check on 1 000 seeded poses (seed `0x11a7`) for each shape, then each analytic gradient matches central differences within 1e-7 relative.
- **AC-3** Given fixture `lin_cylinder.json` (axis free in rx, ry, tx, ty), then the cone rows match the expected values within 1e-15 relative.
- **AC-4** Given a known active set of 4 points for a two-plane zone, then the recomputed s equals ZTYPE usage at the recomputed pose within 1 ulp.
- **AC-5** Given an offset shape with an oracle stub returning known normals, then J_i matches n_iᵀ·∂p_i/∂x exactly for dyadic inputs.

## Design

- **Registry command(s):** none (internal kernel API).
- **Data structures:** `PoseVars { free: DofSet }`; `ZoneLinProblem<'a> { shape, points, drf, vars, oracle, extra }` implementing `LinearisableProblem` and `ActiveSetProblem`; `ExtraConstraints { linear: Vec<Row>, cones: Vec<Cone> }`.
- **Algorithm:** small-angle rotation increment R ≈ I + [ω]× for the linear model, exact rotation (Rodrigues through `fid-num`) for true-objective evaluation; rows built in point-index order.
- **Errors:** `no_free_dof` (caller should use FIXED), `oracle_failed`, `nan_input`.
- **Determinism:** D0 (row construction is fixed order; no reductions other than fixed-order dot products).
- **Performance budget:** row build for 1 M points × 6 DOFs < 100 ms [unverified].
- **Crate:** `fid-gps` (module `zone::pose::lin`).
