# VIZ.RENDER.PREC: Camera-relative precision and far-origin guard

## Purpose

Keep display geometry steady at any distance from the world origin. World positions stay f64 on the CPU; the GPU only ever sees f32 offsets from per-chunk f64 origins, recomposed relative to the camera each frame (relative-to-eye rendering), with a reversed-Z depth buffer. A headless CI guard proves there is no jitter on a far-from-origin scene.

## Scope

**In:** chunk origins and offsets, per-frame relative-to-eye uniforms, reversed-Z depth setup, chunk splitting by extent, the far-origin CI test.
**Out:** LOD selection (`VIZ.RENDER.LOD`); off-screen rendering itself (`VIZ.RENDER.HEADLESS`); coordinate frames and transforms of record (`GEO.FRAME`).

## Requirements

- **REQ-VIZ.RENDER.PREC-001** The renderer shall store each chunk origin as an f64 triple in metres on the CPU and upload vertex positions only as f32 offsets from that origin.
- **REQ-VIZ.RENDER.PREC-002** The chunker shall split geometry so that no uploaded f32 offset exceeds 1 m in magnitude [unverified].
- **REQ-VIZ.RENDER.PREC-003** Each frame, the renderer shall compute every chunk's origin minus the camera eye position in f64 on the CPU and upload the difference as f32.
- **REQ-VIZ.RENDER.PREC-004** The renderer shall use a reversed-Z depth buffer in a 32-bit float format with greater-than depth compare.
- **REQ-VIZ.RENDER.PREC-005** When the far-origin fixture is rendered in a headless sweep, no edge shall move by more than 1 pixel between consecutive frames of a 1 nm camera step [unverified].

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given fixture `fixtures/viz/far-origin-step` (two quads 10 km from the origin, separated by a 1 µm step), when the headless sweep test renders 120 frames at 1920×1080 with the camera moving 1 nm per frame, then the detected step-edge column changes by at most 1 px between consecutive frames.
- **AC-2** (sensitivity control) Given the same fixture rendered with relative-to-eye disabled (absolute f32 positions), when the same sweep runs, then the test reports jitter above 1 px, proving the guard can fail.
- **AC-3** Given a mesh chunk of extent 5 m, when the scene is built, then it is split so that every uploaded offset component is at most 1 m in magnitude.
- **AC-4** (property) Given 10 000 random origins within ±1e7 m and offsets within ±1 m (seeded generator, seed 42), when origin plus f32 offset is recomposed in f64, then the error versus the source f64 position is at most 1.2e-7 m.
- **AC-5** (error path) Given a vertex with a non-finite coordinate, when the scene is built, then the chunk is rejected with error `NonFiniteVertex` and nothing is uploaded.

## Design

- **Registry command(s):** none (internal renderer task).
- **Data structures:** `ChunkOrigin([f64; 3])`; `RteChunkUniform { eye_offset: [f32; 3] }`; `DepthConfig::ReversedZ { format: Depth32Float, compare: Greater, clear: 0.0 }`.
- **Algorithm:** relative-to-eye rendering (Cozzi and Ring, *3D Engine Design for Virtual Globes*, 2011) [unverified]: the view matrix carries rotation only; per chunk, `delta = origin_f64 - eye_f64` is computed in f64 and cast once to f32; the vertex shader adds `delta + offset_f32`. Projection uses an infinite far plane with reversed Z.
- **Errors:** `NonFiniteVertex`, `ChunkTooLarge` (only if splitting is disabled by a test flag).
- **Determinism:** D0 for the CPU origin and delta computation (pure f64, no FMA); pixel output is observed (N) and judged by the 1 px criterion.
- **Performance budget:** per-frame delta upload for 10⁵ chunks under 1 ms on the reference profile [unverified].
- **Crate:** `fid-render` (module `prec`).

## Test fixtures

`fixtures/viz/far-origin-step`: generated by a seeded synthetic generator in the test crate (no external licence). AC-4 uses seed 42. The sweep runs on the wgpu software adapter in CI.

## Build prompt

> In crate `fid-render`, add module `prec` implementing relative-to-eye rendering. Chunk origins are f64 metres held on the CPU; vertices upload as f32 offsets from their chunk origin; split any chunk whose offsets would exceed 1 m in magnitude. Each frame compute `origin - eye` in f64 per chunk and upload it as an f32 uniform; the view matrix holds rotation only. Configure reversed-Z depth (Depth32Float, compare Greater, clear 0.0, infinite far plane). Reject non-finite vertices with `NonFiniteVertex`. Tests: (1) headless sweep over generated fixture `far-origin-step` (two quads at x = 10 km with a 1 µm step), 120 frames at 1920×1080, camera moving 1 nm per frame, assert the step-edge column moves at most 1 px between frames; (2) the same sweep with relative-to-eye disabled must detect jitter above 1 px; (3) a 5 m chunk is split so every offset is at most 1 m; (4) seeded property test (seed 42, 10 000 cases) that recomposition error is at most 1.2e-7 m; (5) non-finite vertex error. Use the headless renderer API from `fid-render::headless`. Rules: no panics, no `unsafe`, deny unwrap/expect/panic/todo/unimplemented/unreachable; no FMA in the CPU delta computation; no value of record is computed here.
