# OUTR.PROG.STDS: Standards-body participation

## Purpose

Gives the project a seat where the formats and rules it depends on are made: QIF, ISO GPS (TC 213), I++ DME and DMIS. It records who represents the project, which drafts were commented on, and which positions were taken, without reproducing any standards text.

## Scope

**In:**
- Target bodies and membership routes
- Representative roles and rotation
- Participation log (meetings, ballots, comments), with designators only
- Feedback loop from spec nodes that hit standards gaps

**Out:**
- Commercial terms, pricing and contracts (business documents outside the tree)

## Requirements

- **REQ-OUTR.PROG.STDS-001** The project shall participate in the relevant standards and interoperability bodies (QIF/DMSC, ISO TC 213 through national mirrors, the I++ group) and publish a participation log with positions taken.
