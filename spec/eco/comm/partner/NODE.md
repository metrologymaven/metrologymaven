# ECO.COMM.PARTNER: Integrator and support-partner program

## Purpose

Open-source metrology software is adopted through integrators who install, train, migrate and support. This node defines how a company becomes a listed partner, what it must know (certification from OUTR.PROG.CERT), how partners escalate defects to maintainers, and how the listing stays honest. Commercial terms between partners and customers stay outside the tree.

## Scope

**In:**
- Listing criteria and renewal
- Required certifications
- Escalation path and defect-report quality bar
- Public partner directory data
- Conflict-of-interest rules for maintainers employed by partners

**Out:**
- Commercial terms, pricing and contracts (business documents outside the tree)

## Requirements

- **REQ-ECO.COMM.PARTNER-001** The project shall publish an integrator and support-partner program (listing criteria, training requirements, escalation path to maintainers) so that organisations can obtain paid deployment and support from listed partners.
