# UNC.ORS.DRF.MOD: Datum references at MMB and LMB

## Purpose

Cases for datum references with material boundary modifiers (MMB, LMB) where the supported subset includes them: the simulator is fixed in size, so the datum feature may shift within it and the frame has a known range of mobility. Expected simulator sizes and shift envelopes are interpretation content for expert authors; agents may generate the geometry with known clearance.

## Scope

**In:**
- Datum features of size at MMB and LMB with constructed clearance
- Expected simulator size, frame and permitted datum shift per dialect
- Case skeletons with geometry generated by `ors_drf` utilities

**Out:**
- Form-perfect cases (owned by `UNC.ORS.DRF.GEOM`); datum features with form error at RMB (owned by `UNC.ORS.DRF.SIM`); format and running (owned by `UNC.ORS.TRACE`, `UNC.ORS.RUN`)

## Requirements

- **REQ-UNC.ORS.DRF.MOD-001** Each material boundary modifier in the supported subset shall have at least one case with constructed clearance between datum feature and simulator.
- **REQ-UNC.ORS.DRF.MOD-002** Every case shall state the expected simulator size, the expected permitted shift (translation in m, rotation in rad) and the dialect, with tolerances.
- **REQ-UNC.ORS.DRF.MOD-003** Every case shall cite the clause and DMT row it exercises.

## Research hypotheses

Expected simulator sizes and permitted shifts are expert content (D-009); the hypotheses below concern only the construction and discrimination power of the cases, so the expert content can be tested once authored.

- **H-1 (construction)** For 100 % of cases, the constructed clearance between datum feature and simulator agrees with the independent oracle (`fiducial-oracles`, Python and mpmath route) within 1e-12 m, measured by `UNC.RDL.GEN.XCHECK` records; falsified if any case disagrees.
- **H-2 (coverage)** Cases exist for 100 % of material boundary modifiers on datum references in the supported subset of the pinned DMT, in each dialect the DMT supports, measured by joining the suite with the DMT; falsified if any is missing.
- **H-3 (discrimination)** At least 90 % [unverified] of cases fail an implementation that treats every datum reference as RMB (no permitted shift), measured by running the cases against that substitute through `UNC.ORS.RUN`; falsified if fewer fail.
- **Reference-set gap:** no public reference data for datum shift was found [unverified].

## Interfaces

- **Offers:** cases for suite `ors-drf` in `ors-case/v1` format.
- **Consumes:** case format and checker (UNC.ORS.TRACE); generators (UNC.RDL.GEN); the pinned DMT as an artifact (GDT.RES).

## Decomposition hints

BOUND (simulator size at MMB and LMB), SHIFT (datum shift and mobility), each an expert content task.

## Open questions

- Interpretation content (expected results that depend on reading a standard, and every DMT row citation) is authored by paid or credentialed experts and signed off by two reviewers, per D-009; agents may draft structure, generators and closed-form mathematics only. Standards texts are provided under D-016.
- How a frame with permitted shift is compared by `UNC.ORS.RUN` (envelope containment rather than a single frame) may need an outcome type in `ors-case/v1` [unverified].
