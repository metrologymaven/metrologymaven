Synthetic test fixtures for spec node OUTR.LAUNCH.PLAN.FMT (CC-BY-4.0).
They are not the project's launch plan: milestone IDs, criteria, roles and the 2099 dates are
made up for the tests. Expected findings are hand-labelled from the spec's rules in
crates/fid-outreach/tests/plan_check.rs; nothing here was produced by running the Rust code.
A fixture directory that lacks PLAN.md or reviews/ uses the one in common/ (no dates, no
review entries); one that lacks apps.toml uses ok/apps.toml.
