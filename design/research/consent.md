## 1. Purpose of this study

You are invited to take part in an open-source user research session for {{FAMILY_NAME}}, a
dimensional metrology software project. The purpose is to learn how practising metrologists,
engineers and shop-floor operators perform inspection, reporting and verification work so we
can improve the design and usability of {{FAMILY_NAME}}.

## 2. What is recorded

During the session, the study observer will record:

- written notes of what you do, say and find confusing;
- task completion outcomes, timings, error counts and assist counts;
- your responses to a ten-question usability questionnaire (System Usability Scale);
- **optional:** audio or screen recording of your {{FAMILY_NAME}} session, only if you agree
  below.

We do **not** record proprietary customer drawings, vendor software screenshots or product
telemetry from your computer unless you separately opt in to telemetry outside this study.

## 3. Storage period and deletion

- Raw session notes, any recordings, this signed consent record and the private code key linking
  your name to your participant code (`P-<study>-<NN>`) are stored **outside the public
  repository** in access-controlled maintainer storage.
- Raw notes, recordings and the code key are kept for at most **12 months** after the session
  date [unverified retention period; confirm in legal review], and are permanently deleted on
  or before that date — or immediately if you withdraw.
- Published research summaries use only your anonymised participant code (`P-<study>-<NN>`) and
  contain no name, e-mail address, phone number or employer name.

## 4. Who sees raw data

Only the named study lead and maintainers directly conducting the research round may access
raw notes, recordings and the private code key. Raw personal data is never sold, never shared
with advertisers or third parties, and never committed to any public repository.

## 5. Right to withdraw at any time

Participation is completely voluntary. You may pause, skip any question or task, or withdraw
from the study at any time — during or after the session — without giving a reason and with no
penalty. If you withdraw, all raw notes, recordings and your entry in the private code key are
deleted promptly. Please note that anonymised aggregate statistics (such as a published release
SUS mean) already published before withdrawal cannot be retroactively un-published, though your
code will not be used in any future publication.

## 6. Separate opt-in for publishing recordings

Publishing anonymised written findings never requires publishing audio or video. Any
publication of a recording or clip requires your **separate, explicit opt-in** below (default
is **No**; choosing **No** does not affect your participation):

- [ ] **No (default):** Do not publish any audio or screen recording from my session; keep any
  recording strictly private for internal analysis and delete it per section 3.
- [ ] **Yes (optional explicit opt-in):** I explicitly allow the project to publish anonymised
  screen-only clips of {{FAMILY_NAME}} from my session (with no voice, face, personal name,
  employer name or proprietary part data visible).
