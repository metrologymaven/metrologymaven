# Contributors

Metrology Maven is built in the open by people and coding agents under the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) and the licences in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## How credit works

- **Code and specification contributors** are credited in Git history (DCO `Signed-off-by`) and release notes.
- **Domain experts** (GD&T interpretation, measurement uncertainty, CMM control — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [D-045](decisions/D-045-provisional-gdt-interpretation.md)) are credited in the spec nodes' `owners:` field, in this file, and in release notes of every release that uses their work.
- **Consent (`REQ-OUTR.GDTC-006`):** No reviewer's personal name or organisation is published here without recorded consent; contributors who prefer a handle or pseudonymous ID are listed by that identifier instead.

## Maintainers and stewards

- **Founder & initial steward:** Metrology Maven (`@metrologymaven`) — see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for governance and [D-052](decisions/D-052-autonomous-zero-human-bottlenecks.md) for autonomous build mode.

## Optional domain reviewers

_Reviewers who optionally sign off provisional interpretation rules (`D-045`) are credited here with recorded consent (`REQ-OUTR.GDTC-006`)._
